# capstone

Signature originals. These rotate at the top of the page.

---

1. If we make it to last call,
   we've already won the night.

2. No such thing as a clean tackle.
   No such thing as a clean round.

3. Here's to the version of me
   that started all this trouble.

4. Drink like a good man leaving town.

5. To the second pour,
   which has never yet disappointed.

6. Half measures finish second.

7. To the one who always gets the last round —
   may your glass be deep and your Ubers cheap.

8. Here's to being wrong loudly
   and laughing about it later.

9. Last call is a suggestion.
   Closing time is a dare.

10. To showing up studs-up.
